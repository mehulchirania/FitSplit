# FitSplit Consent Notice

**Version {{VERSION}} · Last updated {{LAST_UPDATED}}**

This is the short version of what you agree to when you create a FitSplit account. The full details are in the Privacy Notice and the Terms of Service. You must tick the box yourself; it is never pre-ticked.

## 1. What you consent to

> I agree to the Terms of Service and the Privacy Notice, and I consent to FitSplit processing my personal data as described in them. I have read the Health Disclaimer.

By ticking that box you consent to {{OPERATOR_NAME}} processing:

- your account details (name, email, password hash);
- the profile details you choose to add (for example date of birth, gender, height, weight, goals, optional injury or medical notes, profile photo);
- your workout, body-weight, macro and activity logs;
- technical data needed to run and secure the app.

We use this only to run your own FitSplit account: to sign you in, show your plans and progress, let you export or delete your data, keep the service secure and meet legal duties. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. Data is processed by Google (Firebase and Google Cloud) and, for error reports, Sentry, and may be stored or processed outside India. See the Privacy Notice, sections 7 and 8.

## 2. Optional: browser diagnostics

Separate and unticked by default:

> Optional: help us fix bugs by letting FitSplit collect error diagnostics from this device (and occasional masked session replays).

Saying no changes nothing about what you can do in the app.

## 3. If you are under 18

You must also have a parent or legal guardian tick:

> I am this person's parent or legal guardian, and I consent to FitSplit processing their personal data as described in the Privacy Notice.

and enter their email address. We do not track or profile users under 18, and diagnostics are not offered to them.

## 4. Withdrawing consent

You can withdraw any consent at any time, as easily as you gave it:

- the optional diagnostics choice, on the Data Rights page;
- everything, by deleting your account in Settings (your data is erased immediately);
- or by contacting {{CONTACT_NAME}} at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

Withdrawal does not affect processing done before you withdrew. We cannot run your account without the data needed for it, so withdrawing the core consent means closing the account.

## 5. Record of your consent

We store, on your account, the version of the Terms and Privacy Notice you accepted, the time, your age group, your diagnostics choice and, for under-18 users, the guardian's email and the time of their declaration. You can see this in your data export.
