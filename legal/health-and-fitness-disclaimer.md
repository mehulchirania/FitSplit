FitSplit is a workout-planning and logging app. It is not a medical provider, doctor, dietitian, physiotherapist or emergency service, and it does not replace professional advice.

## 1. No medical advice

Workout plans, exercise descriptions and videos, progress charts, body-weight and macro figures, and any other content are for general fitness information only. They are not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment or rehabilitation, and are not tailored to your health.

## 2. Check with a professional first

Speak to a qualified healthcare professional before starting or changing an exercise, nutrition or weight-management programme, especially if you:

- have pain, an injury, illness, disability or a medical condition;
- are pregnant or recently gave birth;
- have heart, blood-pressure, breathing, metabolic or joint concerns;
- take medication that may affect exercise;
- are under 18, or have a history of disordered eating.

## 3. Exercise carries risk

Strength training and cardio can cause injury. Warm up, use a weight you can control, use correct form, and train with a spotter where appropriate. Stop at once and seek help if you feel pain, dizziness, chest discomfort or unusual breathlessness. In an emergency call your local emergency number (112 in India).

## 4. Nutrition and body-weight numbers

Macro targets and calorie figures are estimates, often based on what you type in. They may not suit you. Do not use FitSplit to pursue extreme weight loss or restrictive diets. Contact a registered dietitian if you are unsure.

## 5. Your health notes

Any injury or medical notes you add are optional, are stored only for you, and are not reviewed by anyone at FitSplit. Nobody monitors them or will contact you about them. Do not rely on FitSplit to track a health condition.

## 6. Results and responsibility

We do not guarantee any fitness result. You are responsible for your own training decisions and you use FitSplit at your own risk, subject to the Terms of Service.
